# Database

SQLite is the development store (`data/memora.db`). `DATABASE_URL` can be switched to PostgreSQL later.

Tables include users, caregivers, persons, relationships, known_people, places, events, memories, memory_people, memory_places, memory_events, photos, routines, routine_logs, reminders, activities, activity_results, mood_entries, alerts, conversations, conversation_messages, notifications, and memory_interactions.

Foreign keys and timestamps are used throughout. Demo rows are labeled as synthetic information.
